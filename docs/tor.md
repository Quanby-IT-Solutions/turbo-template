# Terms of Reference (TOR)

**Project Title:** CDC Website Enhancement  
**Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC):** Php 2,000,000.00  
**Mode of Award:** MARB

## I. Project Background

The Clark Development Corporation (CDC) website is envisioned to serve as a comprehensive digital hub that goes beyond traditional marketing and tourism purposes. It will integrate business, tourism, governance, and stakeholder engagement functions, ensuring transparency, accessibility, and a modern user experience.

The website will also function as a central hub for CDC's broader initiatives and responsibilities, including:

1. **Business Opportunities**  
   Presenting investment prospects, information on how businesses can establish and expand their operations, access incentives, and benefit from the economic advantages offered by Clark Development Corporation (CDC). It includes resources for potential investors and bidders interested in participating in CDC's procurement activities, including small value procurement.

2. **Tourism Initiatives**  
   Showcasing CDC's efforts to develop world-class tourist attractions, events, and recreational facilities that enhance visitor experiences. It also provides information for tourists, local communities, and stakeholders on activities, events, and partnerships aimed at boosting Clark's tourism industry and supporting sustainable development.

3. **Announcements, including Issuances and Memo Circulars**

4. **Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Projects**  
   Highlighting CDC's outreach programs, environmental efforts, and socio-economic development initiatives to foster community engagement.

5. **Gender and Development (GAD) Projects**  
   Showcasing CDC's commitment to gender inclusivity, equality, and empowerment.

6. **Job Seeker Resources**  
   Providing updated job openings, career opportunities, and application guidelines to attract qualified talent.

7. **Compliance with Governance Standards**  
   Displaying CDC's adherence to government-mandated transparency requirements, including disclosures related to the Governance Commission for GOCCs (GCG) and other good governance practices. This ensures stakeholders are informed about CDC's activities, financial performance, and strategic plans.

   There are two sections in the website for this purpose:
   - Transparency Seal
   - Corporate Governance Scorecard

8. **Bidding Invitations and Procurement Opportunities**  
   These are disclosures related to procurement opportunities, including those under small value procurement, which cover the following:
   - **Bidding Invitations:** Public announcements inviting eligible bidders to participate in the bidding process.
   - **Bidding Documents:** Detailed materials outlining project requirements, technical specifications, and evaluation criteria.
   - **Notice of Award (NOA):** The formal communication to the winning bidder, confirming their selection for the contract.
   - **Notice to Proceed (NTP):** The official directive to the awarded winning bidder to commence the project or service as specified in the contract.

## II. Objectives

To have an updated website with a Content Management System (CMS) designed to support multiple users with designated roles, such as Content Creators, Content Moderators/Approvers, and Super Admin.

The UI/UX should be modern, sleek, and enable proper display of CDC's compliances.

## III. Scope of Work

1. **Content Audit, Synthesis, and Information Architecture Redesign**  
   The winning bidder shall conduct a comprehensive content audit of the existing CDC website (`www.clark.com.ph`), including all sections such as Transparency Seal, Corporate Governance Scorecard, Bids & Notices, Memo Circulars, Business, Tourism, and other governance and operational disclosures.

   The proponent shall:
   - Analyze, rationalize, and consolidate existing content to eliminate redundancy, outdated materials, and fragmented information.
   - Synthesize and reorganize content into a clear, logical, and user-centered information architecture.
   - Design an intuitive navigation structure that allows users to easily locate information within three (3) clicks or less whenever possible.
   - Develop a simplified menu hierarchy with clear labeling, plain language terminology, and structured categorization of governance, business, tourism, and compliance disclosures.
   - Ensure that Transparency Seal and Corporate Governance Scorecard documents are properly structured, searchable, and organized according to regulatory categories (ARTA, COA, GCG, ISO, FOI, SALN, Procurement).
   - Propose a content taxonomy and tagging structure to enable efficient filtering, searching, and cross-referencing of related documents.
   - Recommend content presentation formats (e.g., dashboards, collapsible sections, document libraries, categorized repositories) to enhance clarity and accessibility.

2. **Newsletter Management Module**  
   The winning bidder shall develop and configure a fully integrated Newsletter Management Module within the CMS to support stakeholder engagement and digital communication.

   The module shall include:
   - Online subscription form accessible from:
     - Homepage
     - News & Events section
     - Website footer
   - Secure subscriber database with:
     - Double opt-in email verification
     - Consent capture compliant with the Data Privacy Act of 2012
   - Manual and scheduled email distribution capability
   - Automated confirmation and unsubscribe mechanism
   - Newsletter archive page accessible on the website
   - Exportable subscriber database in CSV format
   - Administrative dashboard for monitoring subscriber growth and email dispatch history

   The system shall ensure compliance with applicable data protection regulations and prevent unauthorized use of subscriber information.

3. The winning bidder shall submit at least two (2) website design concepts for CDC's approval before front-end development begins.

4. **Develop a fully functional, user-friendly Content Management System (CMS)** with the following user roles:
   - **Content Creator** — Responsible for drafting and submitting content for specific sections of the website. Content creators can upload, edit, and revise articles, images, videos, and other materials assigned to them but cannot publish directly. Their submissions will undergo moderation before becoming publicly visible.
   - **Content Moderator** — Assigned to specific sections of the website. Moderators have the authority to review, approve, request revisions, or reject content submissions within their assigned section only. They cannot publish or modify content in other sections or sitewide. Moderators ensure that content meets quality, branding, and compliance standards before approving it for publication.
   - **Super Admin** — Has full access and control over the entire website. Super Admins can manage all users, assign roles, oversee all content across sections, configure site-wide settings, and perform critical administrative tasks such as backups, security management, and full publishing rights. Super Admins can override approvals and make executive content decisions as necessary.

5. Ensure content migration from the existing website (`www.clark.com.ph`) with data accuracy.
6. Implement security measures following current industry best practices.
7. Conduct system testing (User Acceptance Testing, security checks, performance tests).
8. Deploy the system to CDC's infrastructure with source code turnover.
9. Provide one (1) year of support and maintenance, including bug fixes and updates.
10. Conduct user training and provide documentation for system use and administration.
11. **Compliance with Government Website Standards**  
    The proposed website shall comply with all applicable Philippine government standards, policies, and regulatory requirements governing official government and GOCC websites, including but not limited to:
    - Governance Commission for GOCCs (GCG) disclosure requirements
    - Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA) transparency and Citizen's Charter guidelines
    - Commission on Audit (COA) public disclosure requirements
    - Freedom of Information (FOI) compliance standards
    - Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and National Privacy Commission issuances
    - DICT-issued policies and standards for government websites
    - Accessibility standards for persons with disabilities (WCAG 2.1 Level AA or latest applicable standard)

    The supplier shall ensure that the website structure, document repositories, transparency sections, and public disclosures are organized in accordance with regulatory categories and are easily searchable, accessible, and downloadable.

    The system shall also ensure:
    - Proper document labeling and metadata tagging
    - Mobile responsiveness
    - Accessibility compliance
    - Clear date stamping and archival structure
    - Persistent public access to governance disclosures

## IV. Technology Stack

The proposed system shall be developed using PHP (latest stable version) and MySQL to ensure compatibility with CDC's existing server infrastructure and internal technical support capabilities.

## V. Deliverables

1. **Fully Functional Website** with the following features:

   ### a. User Authentication & Access Control
   - Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) support via email, SMS, or authenticator app
   - Role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring users only access permitted functions
   - Password reset and recovery features

   ### b. Content Management
   - WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get) editor for easy content creation
   - Content revision tracking with version control
   - Draft submission and scheduled publishing options
   - Media library with image/video/document uploading and tagging
   - Defined content submission, review, and approval process
   - Automatic notification system for pending approvals or content feedback
   - Multiple user levels:
     - **Content Creators** — Can create, edit, and submit content for review. Limited access to content management features relevant to assigned sections. Cannot publish content directly; requires approval.
     - **Content Moderators** — Can review, approve, or reject content submitted by Content Creators. Has permission to edit and enhance content before publishing. Can assign content back to creators for revision.
     - **Super Admin** — Full administrative control over system configurations, content approval, and user management. Ability to assign or modify roles and permissions. Access to system-wide settings, including security protocols.

   ### c. Search, Filter, and Categorization
   - Search, filter, and categorization of content entries

   ### d. Dashboard & Reporting
   - Reports and visual analytics for web traffic, content engagement, and user activities
   - Exportable reports for internal review

   ### e. Interoperability
   1. The supplier shall configure and test integration with CDC's existing portals for:
      - Job posting integration with the CDC Human Resource Information System (HRIS) for internal CDC vacancies
      - Structured linking of the Clark Jobs Portal for job postings within the Clark Freeport Zone
   2. Open API structure to allow integration with third-party services such as:
      - Social media platforms
      - Government portals
      - Payment gateways (when applicable)

   ### f. Newsletter Management Module
   - The website shall include an integrated newsletter management module as defined under Section III (Scope of Work)

   ### g. Government Website Compliance

   The fully functional website shall:
   - Meet government transparency and disclosure standards for GOCCs
   - Provide structured sections for:
     - Transparency Seal
     - Corporate Governance Scorecard
     - FOI
     - Citizen's Charter
     - Procurement Transparency
     - Data Privacy Notice
   - Ensure documents are accessible in searchable formats (PDF with selectable text)
   - Maintain structured archives of past disclosures
   - Support accessibility compliance
   - Be compatible with DICT security and hosting standards

2. **Migration of Existing Content**  
   Migration of existing content from `www.clark.com.ph`, ensuring accuracy and retention of structure.

3. **Website Design and Optimization**
   - Modern, sleek, and highly navigable interface with a responsive layout adaptable to desktops, tablets, and mobile devices
   - Intuitive navigation for easy access to content, services, and resources
   - A powerful search feature allowing users to quickly find information
   - Visually cohesive with CDC's branding
   - Optimized page load speeds to ensure fast access

4. **Cybersecurity and Data Protection Requirements**

   ### a. User Authentication and Access Security
   - Secure user authentication using unique user credentials
   - Password policies enforcing:
     - Minimum password complexity
     - Password expiration and reuse rules
     - Automatic account lockout after configurable failed login attempts
     - Session timeout for inactive users
     - Administrative ability to activate, deactivate, or revoke user access

   ### b. Authorization and Access Control
   - Role-based access control (RBAC) to limit system functions and data visibility
   - Restriction of access to confidential and sensitive records based on user role and office
   - Prevention of unauthorized viewing, downloading, printing, or modification of records

   ### c. Audit Trails and Activity Logging
   - Automatic logging of:
     - User logins and logouts
     - Document access and downloads
     - Document creation, modification, referral, and deletion
     - Administrative actions
   - Audit logs shall be:
     - Tamper-resistant
     - Viewable only by authorized administrators
     - Retained based on CDC policy
     - Compliant with standard best practices

   ### d. Data Protection
   - Encryption of sensitive data at rest and in transit within CDC's network
   - Secure storage of documents and metadata
   - Protection against unauthorized data extraction or data leakage

   ### e. System Security Controls
   - Protection against common cybersecurity threats such as:
     - Unauthorized access
     - Malware
     - Data tampering
   - Ability to integrate with CDC's existing security controls (firewall, access policies, etc.)
   - Compliance with applicable government IT security standards

5. **Warranty**
   - The supplier shall provide user support and maintenance services during the warranty period covering one (1) year commencing from the signing of the user acceptance form.
   - User support shall be available during the official working hours of CDC (Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.).
   - User support should cover the following:
     - User assistance
     - Troubleshooting
     - System issue resolution
     - Minor revisions

## VI. Delivery Period

The Supplier shall complete the development, testing, deployment, and turnover of the CDC Website Enhancement Project within four (4) months from the issuance of the Notice to Proceed (NTP).

The four (4)-month period shall include:

- Content audit and information architecture redesign
- UI/UX design approval
- CMS development and configuration
- System integration and testing
- Content migration
- Deployment to CDC Data Center
- User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- Knowledge transfer and documentation turnover

Failure to complete the project within the prescribed period shall be subject to applicable liquidated damages in accordance with government procurement rules.

## VII. Hosting and Deployment

The website shall be hosted exclusively at the CDC Data Center.

The Supplier shall:

- Coordinate deployment with CDC's IT Department
- Conduct on-site system installation at CDC Data Center
- Ensure compatibility and operability within CDC server infrastructure
- Provide complete documentation, including:
  - System architecture
  - User manuals
  - Administrator manuals
  - Backup and recovery procedures

## VIII. Knowledge Transfer

The supplier shall conduct comprehensive system orientation and knowledge transfer sessions for CDC personnel.

## IX. Confidentiality, Ownership and Intellectual Property

a. All project-related information, including user data, system design, source codes, and configurations, is confidential and shall not be disclosed to third parties.

b. As a prerequisite to contract execution, the Winning Supplier shall submit a notarized Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to CDC.

c. Upon turnover and acceptance, the system shall become the exclusive property of Clark Development Corporation.

d. The system or any part thereof shall not be reused, replicated, or deployed without CDC's prior written consent.

e. All administrative access and credentials shall be turned over to CDC upon project completion.
